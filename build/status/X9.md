## Public API (`crate::twin`)
- **Pointer.** Record `twin{tuple, snapshot, reason: "enable"|"owner_refreeze"}`. `tuple::Ptrs.twin` folds it, and so
  does `pin_of(records) -> Pin{tuple, snapshot, at, reason}`.
  - `/rollback twin` (sentinel) now resolves through `Ptrs.twin`.
  - `snapshot::keep_set` pins the twin's snapshot by id.
  - No blob GC exists yet, so tuples need no pin. A future GC must keep `Ptrs.twin`.
- **Freeze.**
  - `tick` freezes the `lkg` tuple, with a fresh system snapshot (`snapshot::take(root, None)`), the first time
    `extend.mode = "free"` holds while an `lkg` exists.
  - If the snapshot fails on `enable`, the tuple is still frozen and the alert `twin_no_state` is raised.
  - An owner re-freeze without a snapshot is refused.
- **Owner lines** (link.rs `line`, `owner.sock` only):
  - `/twin` shows the twin;
  - `/twin refreeze` explains the effect and asks for the phrase;
  - `/twin refreeze confirm` re-freezes.

  No control-link request, grant or record moves the pointer. grant.rs already refuses twin keys.
- **Replay store** `ROOT_HOME/extend/replay/<id>/{entry.json, ws/}`: dirs 0700, files 0600, never mirrored, never in
  the lineage, kept 7 days, at most 64 entries, removed by exact path.
  - `access::workspace_set` calls `session_started(root, Option<snapshot>)`.
  - `link::deliver` calls `relayed`. The first non-slash line of a session that has a workspace snapshot is captured
    (APFS clone of `ROOT_HOME/snapshots/<id>`, on its own thread).
  - Capture runs only on the free path with `twin.enabled`.
  - `sentinel::Hooks.turn_closed` tags the entry with its owner turn's caps.
- **Night** (`tick` from sup.rs every 30 s; the lineage-reading work runs every 10 min):
  - **Window:** 17:00–00:30 UTC, and a pair starts only with 2 × `run_minutes` left.
  - **Sample:** up to k unreplayed tasks from the last 7 days. Tasks whose turn used caps that are flagged (≤ 14 days),
    new since the last report, or `cold`/`archive_due` come first; the rest are random.
  - **Arms:** `run_arm` gives each arm a sealed run root (sealed/run.rs `RunRoot`, sealed profile, own process group)
    with:
    - the tuple's body, ext set and T0 (`snapshot::place_t0`);
    - a clone of the state snapshot's `state/`: *current* = the newest `sys` snapshot, *twin* = the frozen one;
    - a clone of the task's workspace as `INIT_WORKSPACE` with `INIT_ACCESS=workspace`;
    - the live `kernel/config.toml` and the live boot record's model, so both arms run on the current model;
    - no kernel;
    - the original line as the `input` frame;
    - a sealed lane (`twin-<night>-<run>`) with `cap`, `run_requests` (the lane's turn cap) and `run_minutes` (the wall).
  - **Arm order:** random per task.
  - **Outcomes:** `ended` / `capped` / `crashed`, cost, wall, requests, provider errors (ledger status 400/422),
    `mem_cap` kills, and the workspace diff (files and bytes, by size and mtime).
- **Statistics** (exact integers):
  - `two_sided_ppm` is the exact McNemar test on `ended` and the sign test on wall. `cost_test` is the **exact
    Wilcoxon signed-rank test on per-task log cost ratios over the last 14 nights**: the X10 proposal, adopted by the
    orchestrator. It is one-sided `p_ppm` (current costlier) plus `lower_p_ppm`, and works on at most the newest 120
    pairs.
  - `signed_rank_k` and `w_plus` mirror X10's code. Ranks are exact (cross-multiplied max·min in u128), ties keep
    pair order, and zeros are ranked first and counted in n.
  - `regression(last 14 nights)` returns `insufficient` below 40 pairs. Otherwise it returns `no_regression` when
    `ended` is not worse (one-sided McNemar p > 0.05) and the median cost ratio is ≤ 1.5, and `regression` when not.
  - p-values are rounded **up** to ppm, so `p_ppm ≤ 50000` ⇔ p ≤ 0.05 exactly.
- **`twin_report`** (weekly, appended once the week has ended, from Monday 01:00 UTC, while a twin existed). It is
  computed on the newest model among the week's pairs, so reports from before and after a model change stay apart.
  Fields:
  - `week`, `pairs`;
  - `ended{cur, twin, discordant, p_ppm}`;
  - `cost{test:"signed_rank", nights:14, pairs, w_plus, k, p_ppm, lower_p_ppm, median_ratio_permille, cur_higher,
    twin_higher}`;
  - `wall{…sign test…}`;
  - `crashes{cur, twin}`;
  - `spent_musd`, `model`;
  - **`verdict`** (`no_regression` | `regression` | `insufficient`) and **`regression`**, which is true only for
    `regression`, so an insufficient twin never blocks a level (ledger.rs `twin_ok`);
  - `last14{pairs, ended_worse_p_ppm, cost_median_ratio_permille}`.

  **Stop rule:** when 3 consecutive reports have the twin significantly better (`ended`, or cost p ≤ 0.05), the
  alert `twin_worse` fires. **Re-freeze suggestion:** the alert `twin_refreeze_suggested` fires at 90 days, then at
  most once per 30 days.
- **`INIT_HOME/root/twin.json`** (0644, ICJ): the pointer, its age and the re-freeze flag, the budget, the last night
  and its stop reason, the last report and its owner-facing `reading`, the number of stored tasks, and `last14`. It
  holds aggregates only; no task text and no path.
  - `last14` = {pairs, nights, pairs_per_night_permille, run_cost_median_musd, insufficient}: X10's measurement of
    the per-run cost against the $0.025 that 6 pairs a night assume.

## Money (Root-enforced)
- Both arms of a pair get the same lane cap, `min(run_usd, (usd_day − spent tonight) / 2)`.
  - An arm can never spend past its cap, so Σ ≤ `usd_day`.
  - The night stops (`usd_day`) when a pair's cap would fall below $0.01.
- Tonight's spend is re-read from the egress usage ledger: `t:"sealed"` lines of `twin-<night>-…` runs, with orphan
  `start`s counted at their reservation. A Root restart mid-night therefore never spends twice; RF-32 tests this.
- Sealed lanes are charged to `sealed.reserve_usd` and never to the zone's day. Every lane stays under
  `money.total_usd` with no change to money.rs. A lane that refused every request (reserve spent) stops the night
  (`refused`).

## Deviations
1. **Off-peak window** = 17:00–00:30 UTC, a twin.rs constant, not a charter key. It sits inside DeepSeek's discounted
   span every day (egress.md's margins) and is 00:00–07:30 at +07:00.
2. **Per-arm cap** = min(`run_usd`, half of what is left tonight), so both arms of a pair have the same cap and the
   night never passes `usd_day`. With the defaults ($0.30/$0.30) the first pair may use $0.15 an arm.
3. **Twin money comes from `sealed.reserve_usd`** (sealed lanes), on top of the nightly cap. With the default $3.00
   reserve, the twin would use the whole reserve (and so block sealed audits) after 10 to 100 nights. **Owner:**
   size the reserve to include about $9 a month, or approve a separate twin pool (a money.rs change).
4. **Profile:** the arms run under the sealed profile (as X4's probes do), which is narrower than "body + sealed tail,
   home read". A task that reads outside its workspace behaves differently in replay. The workspace path differs from
   the original too: a fidelity limit, beside the ADR's sub-agents limit.
5. **Each stored task is replayed at most once**, so the pairs are independent. A night interrupted by `busy`, `disk` or
   `cancelled` is retried by a later tick inside the window; the other stops end the night.
6. **Store:** the session's workspace snapshot is cloned into the store at capture, because access.rs keeps only 5
   snapshots per workspace. The entry also records the snapshot id.
7. **`ended`** follows sealed runs: the body exits 0, or its last finish was `stop` with nothing in flight for 5 s.
   **`capped`** means the wall, a lane cap (money or requests) or 10 s idle after a cap refusal. **`crashed`** means
   any other exit, or a `mem_cap` kill. A `disk_floor` stops the night.
8. **Report fields** beyond the ADR list: `verdict`, `regression`, `model`, `last14`, `cost.{test, nights, pairs,
   w_plus, k, lower_p_ppm, cur_higher, twin_higher}`, `wall.{cur_higher, twin_higher}`.
9. **Snapshot ids:** a group-less snapshot is `t<ms>`, and now `t<ms>-<n>` when that id is taken (same ms, or a
   frozen fake clock). This is a one-line fix in snapshot.rs's `take`; the full suite had a refreeze refused on
   `snapshot t5000 exists`.
10. **Root size:** twin.rs has about 870 non-blank, non-comment lines against the ADR's ≈ 260 estimate. root.md §4.8
    needs a `twin` sub-budget line (X12; measured + 5%).
