## Public API (for X2, X3, X4, X9, X11)
- **`charter::Extend`** is `[extend]` plus `[extend.twin]`, with C17 bounds. It covers:
  - `mode` and the window (`win_turns`, `win_max_h`, `win_min_h`) and `burst_floor_usd`;
  - the per-level arrays L0…L4: `open_groups`, `changes_day`, `group_max`, `kernel_day`, `background_permille`,
    `bloat_permille`;
  - decay (`cold_turns`, `cold_days`, `archive_turns`, `archive_days`, `archive_debt_days`);
  - the bloat budget at L0 (`bloat_caps`, `bloat_t0_kib`, `bloat_ext_mib`, plus `bloat_boot_permille` and
    `bloat_body_permille`, which wait for the twin);
  - `snap_max_gib`, and the twin keys.

  The section is left out of the effective JSON at its defaults, so digests do not change. `Extend::budget(level) ->
  Budget{open_groups, changes_day, group_max, kernel_open, kernel_day, background_permille, bloat_permille}`.
  `charter::check(&Charter, &Ctx)` re-validates C2–C17.
- **The effective charter.** `st.charter` is the active charter with the owner's grant overlay on top
  (`grant::merged`, at Root start and in `apply_charter`). The digest stays the file's. Money, quotas,
  `autonomy.max_level`, action classes and `[extend]` therefore follow grants with no change on the reader's side.
- **Grants** (`grant::`):
  - `request(root, args)` is the control link's `grant.request`;
  - `decide(root, id, approve, hours, confirm)`;
  - `line(root, text)` handles the owner lines `/grants`, `/grant <id> [for <n>d|<n>h] [confirm]`, `/deny <id>` and
    `/release <cap>`;
  - `tick` (expiry, run from `ledger::tick`);
  - the accessors `budget(root, level)`, `snap_max_gib(root)`, `win(root, kernel)`, `burst_floor_musd(root)`,
    `dep_covers(root, crates)` and `workspace_writers(root)`.
- **The ledger** (`ledger::`):
  - `Level` (the §7 fold), `Step`, `tick`, `anchor(root)` (`"settled"` on the free path, `"audited"` on gated);
  - `NO_ROLLBACK`;
  - `promotion_closed(records, sha)`;
  - `verify(records)`, also run by `init-root ledger --verify`.
- **Selection** (`select::`):
  - `register()` adds `Hooks.admit` (inserted first), `Hooks.turn_closed` and `Hooks.direct`;
  - `admit`, `turn_closed`, `direct`, `release`, `tick`;
  - the pure `decay`, `fisher_tail`, `correlate`, `bloat_check`, `budget`, `sizes`;
  - `view(root)`, the X6 part of `extend.json` (X3's view does not call it yet).
- **Records.**
  - New Root kind `turns{day, start, owner, background, owner_harmed, background_harmed}`, one per owner day on the
    free path.
  - `cap_state` gains `until` on quarantines and `stats{owner_turns, days}` on decay.
  - `harm{signal:"quarantine_debt", action:"demote", caps, rollback}`.
  - `grant_request{id, kind, value, duration_h, why_sha256}`.
  - `grant{id, kind, value, until, decision}`.

## Done
- **X7 levels (§7.1, §7.2).** A pure fold over Root-written records only:
  - owner turns, from `turns` days that started at or after the entry record;
  - days at the level;
  - clean groups: `change_close` `clean`, at least 5 turns, and a tuple that differs from its parent's;
  - direct harm rollbacks in the last 30 closed groups;
  - the baseline ring;
  - demotions in the last 7 days;
  - quarantines in the last 14 days;
  - consecutive clean twin reports.

  `promotion`, gate records and zone usage never enter, which closes RT-GAP-1 by construction. The L1–L4 table is
  ADR-011 §7.2's.
- **Demotion (§7.3).**
  - Free path: a demoting refusal or an `escapee` (H4), `/panic`, a crash loop on lkg, quarantine debt after 72 h, and
    `harm_rate` (direct rollbacks above 2× the level's threshold). `harm_rate` and quarantine debt append their own
    `ladder down` with no rollback (cause `harm_rate`, or `demotion`).
  - Gated path: today's triggers, plus `harm_rate`.
  - Demotion and panic resume roll back to `ledger::anchor`.
- **The free path** refuses a kernel `promotion{lkg:true}` that matches no closed group (X3's Need; the genesis one is
  exempt).
- **`ledger --verify`** re-checks every climb against §7's terms at its moment, and every demotion against a pending
  trigger.
- **Grants (§7.4).**
  - Allowed kinds: `money.day_usd` and `money.total_usd`, `quotas.parallel_bodies`, `extend.open_groups`,
    `extend.changes_day`, `extend.bloat_factor`, `extend.background_share`, `extend.snap_max_gib`,
    `fleet.workspace_write`, `dep`, `autonomy.max_level`, and `actions.<class>` below the class's `min_level`.
    Anything else gets `root.args`.
  - At most 3 requests per rolling 24 h. The `why` is kept in `state/grants/<id>.json` and only its sha256 goes into
    the lineage.
  - `/grant` needs the typed confirmation (`access::PHRASE`). The overlay is `state/grants.toml` (0600), with an
    expiry that defaults to the request's duration, else 30 days, and is at most 365 days.
  - The overlay is re-validated by C1–C17: an entry that would make the charter invalid is refused, or skipped at load.
  - Expiry appends `grant{decision:"expired"}`.
- **Charter `[extend]` and C17.** See above, plus the `extend` digest on the `charter` record.
- **Broker members at L0.** On the free path, members up to `quotas.parallel_bodies` (with grants) are allowed at every
  level. Going past the limit is a `root.quota` refusal, not a demoting `root.level` one.
- **X6 decay and archive (§6.2).** At each owner-day boundary:
  - `active` → `cold` → `archive_due`, and back on use;
  - archive debt after `archive_debt_days` refuses changes that add caps;
  - the zone's move is recorded as `archived`.
- **X6 quarantine (§6.3).**
  - The one-sided Fisher tail, exact in u128 (C(120,60) checked), at P ≤ 0.01/n_tested, a ≥ 4 and a 2× ratio, once a
    day. A cap used in every turn is `untestable`.
  - Direct rollbacks quarantine through `Hooks.direct`, including the 30-day repeat ban.
  - While the quarantined version is live, A5 refuses every change that does not remove it.
  - A new version is banned for 7 days, or 30 for a second quarantine within 30 days. The quarantined version itself
    stays banned until `/release`.
  - At 72 h: R1 to the newest tuple without the version if it is at most 14 days old (`sentinel::rollback`), else the
    `quarantine_stuck` alert. Either way it records the demoting `harm`.
- **X6 bloat (§6.4).** Active non-core caps, the `t0` blob and the ext set, against the L0 budget × the level's factor.
  Over budget, a change that grows a quantity is refused (`extend.bloat`, which lists cold caps); one that shrinks or
  keeps it passes.
- **Owner-corrections trend** (X10 item 5; orchestrator, 2026-10-22). This is a Root alert that blocks level-ups and
  rolls nothing back.
  - Reference: the first 14 free-path owner days, with at least 40 owner turns.
  - Window: the last 14 owner days after the reference, with at least 40 owner turns.
  - It fires when two things hold:
    - the window's rate is ≥ 1.5 × r0, where r0 = max(50‰, (c_ref + 1)/(n_ref + 2));
    - the exact one-sided Fisher tail is ≤ 1/10,000 (the window's corrections among both spans; `sentinel::stats::fisher_tail`).
  - The `turns` record gains `owner_corrected`, and the ledger folds the trend from those records, so `ledger --verify`
    recomputes it.
  - Every climb gets a criterion `corrections_trend` (`have` = window ‰, `need` = r0 ‰).
  - When it fires, selection appends `harm{signal:"corrections_trend", action:"alert", value, threshold, p_ppm}` and the
    alert `corrections_trend`, once per episode. It never demotes.
  - Vectors: `tests/fixtures/x67/trend.py` writes `trend.json` (15 cases, Python fractions), and
    `ledger::tests::trend_vectors` matches them field for field.
  - Measured at 32 owner turns a day:
    - null, corrections at 10%: **0 alert episodes in 20 × 365 days**;
    - 10% → 35% over 6 weeks (X10's corrections salami): **fired in 20/20 seeds, median day 20, latest day 31**.
  - A one-sample binomial test against r0 was tried first. It fired 11 times in those 20 null years, because a
    reference that is low by chance looks like a rise. The two-sample test carries the reference's sampling error.
- **X6 admission:** `change.open` is refused on `"gated"` (`root.mode`, `reason: "gated"`; X3's Need), before every
  other check.

## Deviations
1. **The ladder is §7's on both paths.** ADR-011 redefines ADR-007 decision 3 without a path qualifier, and RT-GAP-1
   must close everywhere. On `"gated"` there are no change groups, so the ladder cannot climb past L0 there. That
   matches a release build's `SUPPORTED_MAX_LEVEL = 0`.
   - Demotion triggers do depend on the path: gated keeps today's (sealed audit, mutation, gate canary, body safe), so
     WP24's and WP25's demotion tests stay as they are.
   - The `[ladder]` charter section is still parsed and validated (digest stability), but it is no longer read.
2. **Owner turns come from a new Root kind, `turns`** (one record per owner day). No lineage record carried owner turns,
   and `ledger --verify` must recompute levels. A day counts toward a level only if it **started** at or after the
   entry record. That undercounts by at most the entry day, which is conservative.
3. **"The baseline ring is full"** is read from records: at least 40 owner turns in days that started after the newest
   non-clean group close. This is a conservative proxy for X3's ring that the lineage can recompute; the live ring is
   not read.
4. **`harm_rate`** is evaluated at each Root `direct` close, from L1 up, against twice the threshold of the level the
   agent is at. Being edge-triggered, it costs at most one level per harmful close.
5. **Twin "no regression":**
   - X9's `regression` flag is used when present;
   - otherwise a report needs at least 40 pairs, a median cost ratio ≤ 1500‰, and `ended` not worse at p ≤ 0.05;
   - "the last N reports" means the newest N overall.
6. **Archive debt starts after the 7-day grace.** "Must be moved within 7 days … until then" is read as: refuse adding
   caps once 7 days have passed and the cap is still unmoved.
7. **Quarantine release state.** `/release` records `to:"active"` while the version is still live, else `to:"archived"`:
   the cap sits in `quarantine/`, outside the `t0` blob.
8. **The prompt files `prompt/GENESIS.md` and `prompt/SELF.md`, and caps seen only in use (`observed`), are treated as
   core.** They never decay and are not counted for bloat. There is no genesis `core` list yet.
9. **Bloat:** boot prompt tokens and the body binary are measured against the twin, so they wait for X9's `twin` tuple.
   They are not measured yet.
10. **Grants:**
    - A per-level budget grant raises every level to at least its value. A scalar grant replaces the charter value.
    - When several grants of the same kind are live, the later one wins until it expires.
    - `dep` and `fleet.workspace_write` are stored and exposed (`dep_covers`, `workspace_writers`), but the broker and
      the `dep` admission do not read them yet.
11. **Request ids** are `g` followed by 8 hex characters, so they are short to type. Their format is checked before any
    path is built.
12. **`select` state.** Owner-turn end times (the last 4,096), each cap's last use and the open day's counts are in
    `ROOT_HOME/state/select.json`. Quarantines, archive debts and registrations are folded from the lineage at load
    and at each daily evaluation.
