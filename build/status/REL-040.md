## Decisions
- `promote --check` is a flag on `promote` (`check: true`), not a new command: the lane and level rules of promote
  apply as they are; it is intercepted before the state gating and the quota ticket, since it promotes nothing.
- A re-file supersedes only the goal's **newest** prediction and only while its change has never gone live; a re-file
  after the change went live is still `goal.late`. The superseded prediction gets no outcome record (calibration folds
  outcomes only), and the closure is on the new prediction's lineage record (`supersedes`).
- `met_unattributed` is a Root-recorded reason, not one the agent can claim; the agent's six stop reasons are
  unchanged. A threshold not reached stays `met`, `verified: false`, as before.
- The meter counts what settles while the goal is active. The requests of the stop turn that settle before the agent's
  `goal stop` are the goal's; the rest of that turn (the final reply) settles after the goal stopped and is not. The
  reconciliation is over the goal's active time.
- The CI rerun after 4a76b12 covered the Root steps only: the change is one attribute line's position in root `sys.rs`.
- Previously published `research_notes/` and `reports/` files stay as they are in the public tree (excluded from the
  sync, neither changed nor removed); whether to remove them from the public repo is the owner's call.
