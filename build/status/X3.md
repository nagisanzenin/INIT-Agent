# X3: the Root harm sentinel (ADR-011 §3)

Status: done (Root side), 2026-10-22 · lane X3-SENTINEL · root `src/sentinel/` (new) · commits 076544f, 52195ea, 18195ba, 2766612, 96cb302, f5e9f3a, 2db355a, 9c2c746, c539cb8, 1496814, 8bb3848

## Public API (binding for X4/X5/X6/X7; X4 and X5 built against it)

Module `crate::sentinel` (`root/crates/init-root/src/sentinel/{mod.rs, engine.rs, stats.rs, tests.rs}`). Its state is
`Root.sentinel: sentinel::Shared`, a field in `link.rs`. Conventions:
- Times are Root-clock ms (`util::now_ms`), so the fake clock drives everything.
- Money is integer micro-USD.
- Every statistic is exact integer combinatorics: `stats.rs`, with a small unsigned bignum and no floats.
- `engine.rs` is a pure state machine. It is fed observations with explicit times and returns actions, which `mod.rs`
  executes with no lock held. The sentinel lock is a leaf.

### Shared files (one-line hooks; all committed)
- `main.rs`: `mod sentinel;`.
- `lineage/kinds.rs`: all twelve ADR-011 Root kinds at once.
- `link.rs`:
  - the `sentinel` field;
  - `deliver`, which relays an owner line or an interrupt (after its locks drop);
  - `ack`;
  - `line`, which handles `/rollback`, placed after `/panic` and before the access lines;
  - `after`, which sees every Root-appended record;
  - the `/root help` text.
- `sup.rs`:
  - `Ev::Restart(Plan)`;
  - `Plan.target = Some("parent")`, resolved by `sentinel::rollback_to`;
  - `change.open` dispatched off the reader thread, like `sealed.g4_slice`;
  - `change.veto` in `handle`;
  - the `spawn` count, and `killing` before a kernel `kill`;
  - `hb{busy}`;
  - `exited` (body instances);
  - kernel `body_rollback` records;
  - the run-loop tick.
- `egress.rs`: `egress_start` after the live `start` record, and `egress_end` after the `end` record. X5's `req_caps`
  runs earlier in `finish_with`.
- `memwatch.rs`: the zone sink also feeds `mem_event` (H6).
- `panic.rs`: `panicked`, after the panic records. They are appended under the panic's own lineage lock, so `after`
  misses them.

### Control link (root.md §4.1.3, ADR-011 Records)
- **kernel → Root `change.open`.** The request is `{sha, from_sha, scope, candidate{body:{binary, sha, t0?}, ext:{components, set}, kernel?}, caps, prefix_change}`;
  the answer is `{seq, group, snapshot, window:{turns, max_h, min_h, quiet}}`. The sentinel does, in order:
  1. Validates the request.
  2. Runs A0: the candidate's blobs are in the store with their digests, and a charter is active.
  3. Calls `Hooks.caps`, X5's change caps (otherwise the zone's caps, tagged `evidence:"zone"`).
  4. Runs `plan`, X3's part of A5:
     - the open groups, changes per owner day and `group_max` against §4.5 by level;
     - the H5a freeze;
     - kernel changes: L1, no open group, the per-day limit;
     - no change while a rollback is pending;
     - re-admission (§3.5).
  5. Calls every `Hooks.admit` with `args.group` and `args.parent_tuple` added. These are X4's A4 and snapshot, and X6's checks.
  6. Saves the candidate tuple (the current kernel plus the candidate body, ext and t0).
  7. Appends `change_open{caps, checks{A0,A4,A5}, from_sha, group, level, open_groups, parent_tuple, readmit, scope, sha, snapshot, tuple}`,
     then `tuple` (so `current` moves).
  8. Sends `extend.window{group, state:"open"}`.
- **kernel → Root `change.veto{group, reason}` → `{}`** (H12). It is always honoured and never attributed.
- **Root → kernel `extend.rollback{to, groups, reason}`.** The kernel confirms with its existing
  `lineage.append body_rollback{from_sha, to_sha, reason}`, where `to_sha` is the target tuple's `body.sha`. The Root
  then appends `rollback{from, to, target:"parent", reason}`. With no confirmation within 120 s, or with no kernel
  running, the Root does the whole-system rollback of root.md §3.7.3 through `Ev::Restart` with boot reason
  `rollback_system`. An H1 escalation also calls `Hooks.restore` (X4's R2) once the zone holds nothing.
- **Root → kernel `extend.window{group, state:"open"|"closed", outcome?}`.**
- **Owner line `/rollback [sha|cap|settled|lkg]`** (a Root line):
  - with a sha or cap: H3 on the named group, or on the newest group when no argument is given;
  - `settled` or `lkg`: closes every group as collateral and does a whole-system rollback to that pointer;
  - `twin` waits for X9's pointer.

  A relayed `/reject <sha|cap>` is also H3.

### Hooks (`sentinel::hooks()`, a `Mutex<Hooks>`, read and copied before any call)
`admit: Vec<AdmitFn>` (X6 first, then X4), `caps: Option<CapsFn>` (X5), `turn_closed: Vec<fn(&Root, &Turn)>` (X5, X6),
`direct: Vec<fn(&Root, &[String], bool)>` (X6: the caps of a `direct` group, or a cap the owner named; `true` means the
repeat ban applies, i.e. two direct rollbacks within 14 days → 30 days), `restore: Option<RestoreFn>` (X4).

### Functions for other lanes
- `req_caps(root, req_id, caps)`: X5's tap. `root` evidence beats `zone`, and the uses are summed.
- `turns(root)`: the last 120 closed turns, owner and background (X6's Fisher test).
- `turn_caps(root)`: at most 16 ids from the open turn (X5's `egress_action`).
- `open_groups(root)`.
- `rollback(root, to, reason)`: R1 with the 120 s fallback (X6's 72 h quarantine rollback).
- `view(root)`: X3's part of `INIT_HOME/root/extend.json`: groups and windows, the freeze, the rings' fill, a pending
  rollback. It is written only on the free path, or while a group is open.
- `Turn{seq, kind, start, end, cost_musd, wall_ms, requests, req_bytes, provider_errors, correction, crash, kill, refusal,
  capped, zspend_musd, caps, groups, tuple}` with `harmed()`.
- `stats::{exceed_tail, exceed_k, binom_tail, binom_k, fisher_tail, Big, Tail::{le, ppm}}`. X6 can reuse `fisher_tail`
  (RF-10 checks it against Python).

## Done
- **Turns (§3.1).**
  - An owner turn starts when an owner line is relayed to a ready kernel.
  - A background turn starts at zone egress outside owner turns.
  - A turn ends after 30 s with nothing in flight, or at the 15-minute cap (`capped`).
  - When the owner speaks, a background turn ends and its in-flight requests move to the owner turn.
  - Per turn: cost, wall, requests, request bytes, provider errors, correction, crash, kill, refusal, caps, the open
    groups and the tuple.
  - Corrections: an interrupt; a steer; a line whose first word is `/undo`, `/stop`, `/reject`, `/rollback` or
    `/panic`. A Root-handled correction typed within 5 minutes of a turn's end goes to that turn.
- **Rings (§3.2).**
  - The turn ring holds 40 owner turns, the request ring 400 requests.
  - A turn enters the rings only when every group open at its start closed `clean`, or when none was open and its
    tuple is clean. Turns served during a window wait in `pending`. This is the salami defence.
- **Exact tests.**
  - The exceedance test (m = 40, j = 10) and the binomial upper tail, both exact.
  - The binomial tail steps its terms with exact bignum division, so H9 is exact at hundreds of requests.
  - Thresholds are cached.
  - The magnitude guards use twice-medians: 1.5× for cost and wall, 2× for requests, 3× for bytes.
- **Signals.**
  - H1 (body crashes ≥ 2 in 600 s, or one kernel crash, with a group open; R2 escalation).
  - H2 (deafness: ack 30 s, idle 120 s, any 900 s, 2 in a row).
  - H3 (`/reject`, `/rollback`, `/panic`).
  - H4 (a demoting `ceiling_hit`, `escapee`).
  - H5a (60 min ≥ max($0.50, 4 × p95); freeze 1 h).
  - H5b, H7, H8, H10, H11 (looks at n = 5, 10, 20, α = 0.002).
  - H6 (2 `mem_cap` in 24 h; a `disk_floor` with growth ≥ 2 GiB; > 120 spawns per hour).
  - H9 (≥ 3 errors, binomial at max(20‰, (b+1)/402)).
  - H12 (veto).
  - S1 at close gives `flagged`.
- **Groups and windows.**
  - A change made in the same turn joins the open group; a new version of a cap replaces the old one.
  - The window runs from the last join: 20 turns, 72 h, at least 2 h (kernel: 40, 120 h, 24 h).
  - Fewer than 5 turns at close gives `quiet`.
  - `lkg` moves to the newest closed `clean`, `quiet` or `flagged` tuple once older groups have closed and no rollback
    is pending.
  - Open groups, outcomes and re-admission history are re-folded from the lineage at load. The rings and windows
    persist in `ROOT_HOME/state/sentinel.json` (0600).
- **Attribution (§3.4) and re-admission (§3.5).**
  - Churn signals take every open group: `direct` if it is alone, `suspect` otherwise.
  - Chronic signals and H5a take the newest group, `direct`.
  - H3 and H12 take the named group; newer groups go as `collateral`. H12 is never attributed.
  - Re-admission: `suspect`, `collateral` and vetoed changes once each, alone, not counted against the day. `direct`
    changes never.
  - The repeat ban passes to X6.
- **`extend.mode`.** Groups exist only on the free path, so every group signal acts in either mode. The system-level
  actions taken with no group open (H2's restart, H5a's alert) need `extend.mode = "free"`. That is X7's key; when it
  is absent the mode is gated.

## Deviations
1. **A0 is partial.** It checks the candidate's blobs and digests and that a charter is active. The Root re-hashing its
   own binary against `root_start` is not done: no `root_start` record exists in this Root (the kind is registered,
   but nothing appends it).
2. **H6 growth uses a proxy.** "INIT_HOME grown ≥ 2 GiB since the oldest open group opened" is measured as the drop in
   free space on the `INIT_HOME` volume, read with `statvfs` at the group's open and at the `disk_floor` event.
   Walking `INIT_HOME` with `du` at every event would be too slow.
3. **H5a's p95** is taken over the hours of a rolling 14 × 24 h window that had spend, rather than over "14 owner days".
4. **Turn wall** runs from the turn's start to its last request's end, or to the cap. The 30 s quiet tail is not counted.
5. **S1's "zone spend per turn"** is a per-owner-turn value: each owner turn is charged its own cost plus the background
   spend since the previous owner turn. It is tested like cost (exceedance at α = 0.05).
6. **Window turns and H9.**
   - The window counts only owner turns that started at or after the group's last join. The turn the change was made in
     is excluded, because its building cost would bias the cost tests.
   - H9 uses the window's requests from every turn, capped at the newest 4,096.
   - Kernel windows look at n = 5, 10 and 20, like body windows.
7. **The H4 action.** With groups open, H4 closes them as `collateral` and rolls back to the oldest group's parent.
   The demotion itself, the rollback to `settled`, is the ledger's (X7). For `escapee`, the ledger must also treat it as
   a demotion trigger (see Needs).
8. **`/panic`** closes the open groups as `collateral`, with no event, because the kernel is dead. Panic resume's
   rollback (to `settled`, X7) does the rest.
9. **Re-admission scope.** A change rolled back by H12 (`vetoed`) is re-admissible once, like `suspect`. A
   re-admission needs no group open, and it opens its own group.
10. **`change.open` in gated mode** is accepted (see Needs: X7). Today's built kernel never sends it.
11. **Late caps.** If a request's turn has already closed (the 15-minute cap), its late `req_caps` updates only
    `turns()`'s copy (X5 deviation 10).

## Tuning of 2026-10-22: H5b and the clustering fix (orchestrator, under the owner's mandate; commits 9c2c746 and the one after it)
- **H5b.** The threshold is now the 8th largest ring value (`RING_J_COST = 8`) at α = 0.005 (`HARD_COST`), giving
  K ≥ 5/5, 7/10, 11/20. This was X10's proposal. H8, H10, H11, H7, H9 and S1 are unchanged.
- **Session units: the clustering fix.** X10 found that turns of one task share its difficulty, so they are not
  exchangeable. On X10's clustered model the per-turn tests rolled back 9.7–14.8 % of null windows.
  - What a unit is: consecutive owner turns of one owner session. `workspace.set` starts a session, through a
    one-line hook in `access.rs`. A unit ends at a gap of 30 min or more, or at 6 turns (`UNIT_MAX`, the fallback for
    long sessions). Every `change.open` commit starts a new unit, so no unit spans a change.
  - How a unit is scored: one value, ⌊(Π(x+1))^(1/n)⌋ − 1, an exact integer geometric mean (`engine::gm1`, on
    `stats::Big`).
  - What runs on units: H5b, H8, H10, H11 and S1's cost, wall and zspend. They compare the window's units with the
    ring's newest 40 units.
  - What stays per turn or per request: H7 runs per turn (r0 over every turn of the ring units), and H9 per request.
  - Gating: every chronic test waits for 40 ring units. The looks stay at 5, 10 and 20 window turns, with K and the
    thresholds at n = the window's unit count.
- **Measured** with `rf11_full_size` (20,000 null windows and 10,000 power windows per row; ± one standard error):

  | Model | Null false rollback (all chronic signals) | 3× cost caught by H5b |
  |---|---|---|
  | iid, every turn its own session | 1.59 % ± 0.09 | **67.3 %** ± 0.5 |
  | iid, one long session (units of 6) | 0.68 % ± 0.06 | **75.3 %** ± 0.4 |
  | sessions of 4, no shared offset | 0.95 % ± 0.07 | 64.4 % ± 0.5 |
  | clustered L = 4, σ_s = 0.3 | 1.00 % ± 0.07 | 55.4 % ± 0.5 |
  | clustered L = 4, σ_s = 0.6 (X10's headline; was 9.7 %) | **1.27 %** ± 0.08 | 40.0 % ± 0.5 |
  | clustered L = 8, σ_s = 0.6 (was 14.8 %) | **2.38 %** ± 0.11 | 38.8 % ± 0.5 |

  Every null row is ≤ 2.5 %. Power is ≥ 60 % on the independent-turn models. X10's mirror (fcce882) agrees within
  noise.
- **Tests.**
  - `rf11_null_streams_false_rollback_at_most_2_5_percent` covers both iid models, null and power, at 3,000 and 1,500
    windows.
  - `rf11_clustered_sessions_keep_the_null_bound` covers the three clustered models. It replaces the open-risk test,
    with its assertion flipped.
  - `units_follow_sessions_gaps_and_changes` covers unit formation and `gm1`.
  - `x10_judge_vectors` now also checks X10's `units` section in full: all 44 cases, every look's hits, the unit counts,

- **Open trade-offs for the owner** (X10.md has the grid):
  1. Under clustering, 3× power falls to 38–40 %, below the 60 % target. Only longer windows recover it: 30 turns gives
     59 %, 40 turns gives 72 %. But with 8-turn sessions those windows push the null back above 2.5 % (3.7 %).

## v2_fix: chronic signals in long sessions (orchestrator decision, 2026-10-22)
**The gap X10 found.** In one long owner session (gaps under 30 min), a 20-turn window forms only 4 units
(6, 6, 6, 2).
- At j = 10 and α = 0.002, "4 of 4" has tail 0.0053 > 0.002, so H8, H10 and H11 could never fire there. At 3×, X10
  measured wall power at 0.7 %.
- The 5- and 10-turn looks hold 1–2 units, so in long sessions only the 20-turn look is live.

**Decision: v2_fix is adopted.** H8, H10 and H11 now use H5b's rank and α: the 8th largest of the 40 ring units at
α = 0.005, so "4 of 4" is reachable (tail 0.0024).
- The `live` block's `k_wall`, `k_req` and `k_bytes` follow the same rank.
- S1 keeps j = 10 at α = 0.05. H7 keeps α = 0.002.
