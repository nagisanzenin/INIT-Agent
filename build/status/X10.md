### Defaults that fail or need changing

1. **The twin's weekly cost test (exact sign test over the week's 42 pairs) fails the salami row on any reading that
   is not inflated by multiple looks.** The week-6 report catches the reference salami only 48.8% of the time. Under
   the literal "any week ≤ 6" reading it passes (83.5%), but that reading also flags 25.7% of harmless agents.
   - **Proposal: the exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test on per-task log cost ratios, over the last 14 nights.** That is
     the same span "no regression" already uses. Integer counts, u128, n ≤ 120.
   - The week-6 catch rate rises to 84.1%, and the weekly null alarm stays at 4–5%.
   - Cost: adjacent reports share a week, so the stop rule's cost half (3 consecutive bad weeks) false-alarms about
     7.4% a year instead of 0.9%. Make it 4 consecutive if that is too high.
   - The proposal still needs two things:
     - **σ_w ≤ 1.** At σ_w = 0.5, even the ADR's sign test reaches 93.8%.
     - **The full k = 6 pairs a night.** At 3 pairs a night the proposal reaches only 56%.

     $0.30 a night buys 6 pairs only if a replay run costs ≤ $0.025. The ADR estimates $0.01–0.10 per run, so at
     $0.05 the twin gets 3 pairs a night.
   - **Measure σ_w and the per-run cost in the first 14 nights**, as twin-vs-twin A/A replays, before trusting this
     row.
2. **H5b (cost per turn) passes 3×, but only just.** It catches 61.2%, with a lower bound of 60.55%. The pass also
   assumes σ = 1: with per-turn cost σ = 1.5 the 3× catch rate drops to 23.9%.
   - **Proposal: H5b alone uses the 8th largest of the 40-turn ring and α = 0.005**, so K ≥ 5 of 5, ≥ 7 of 10,
     ≥ 11 of 20 (α = 0.004 gives the same table).
   - 3× → 68.1%, 2× → 27.3% (was 20.4%), null +0.31 points (1.49% → 1.80%).
   - H8, H10, H11, H7, H9 and S1 are unchanged.
   - Other options measured on the 3× row:
     - a shared j = 20: 48.7% (3,000 windows);
     - a shared j = 8 or 12 at α = 0.002: about 54% or 47% (Python approximation);
     - an extra look at n = 15: 62.0% (10,000 windows);
     - α = 0.0005: 30.8% (10,000 windows).
3. **The exceedance tests assume exchangeable turns, and real turns cluster in sessions.** No constant fixes this; it
   needs a design change, and it is the biggest risk found.
   - Measured null false rollback per window:
     - sessions of 4 turns sharing a log offset of sd 0.3 (ICC 0.09): **3.5%**;
     - sd 0.6 (ICC 0.36): **9.7%**;
     - sessions of 8 turns at sd 0.6: **14.8%**.
   - Lowering α does not repair it: α = 0.0002 still gives 3.2%, and 3× power falls to 31%.
   - Recommendation, before `extend.mode = "free"` becomes the default:
     - **measure the ICC of cost, wall, requests and bytes within owner sessions** from the first 14 days of Root turn
       records;
     - if it is ≥ 0.05, **move the chronic tests' unit to the owner session** (one value per session, in the ring and
       the window), or calibrate K by a session-block permutation of the ring.

     This is an owner or ADR decision (open question). X3 logged it in build/status/X3.md.
4. **The ADR's claim "the baseline ring … does not drift down with them" (Threat model, salami) does not hold.**
   Salami groups close `clean`, so their turns enter the ring. The ring's median cost drifts +61% in 6 weeks, in step
   with the agent.
   - The sentinel rolls back salami groups at the null rate (1.8% per group). The twin is the only defence, hence
     item 1.
   - Suggestion: keep a second, frozen magnitude baseline from `settled` or the twin era (ADR-010's S2 drift ring,
     which ADR-011 moved to the twin).
5. **A corrections salami (a hider) is invisible to every mechanism.** It adds +1 correction point per group with no
   cost change.
   - Corrections rise 10% → 35% in 6 weeks, or → 60% at +2 points per group.
   - The sentinel rolls back runs only slightly more often than the null does: 48% of runs have any rollback, against
     38% for the null. The ring drifts with the agent as in item 4.
   - The twin cannot see it: its replays score `ended`, cost and wall only.
   - The live trajectory's "corrections per turn are not rising" is report-only.
   - Suggestion: make it a Root alert that blocks level-ups, using an exact binomial test of the last 14 days against
     the first 14 days' reference.

## API (`kernel_gate::stats::extend_sim`)
- `exact`: `Big`, `binom`, `Tail{le, ppm}`, `exceed_tail`/`exceed_k`, `binom_tail`/`binom_k`, `fisher_tail`, and
  `Pascal::fisher_le` (u128, N ≤ 120). Also `median2`, `jth_largest`, `max_rate`.
- `Defaults::{adr, proposed}`: every knob under test.
- `judge` and `soft`: mirrors of the Root's `engine::judge` and `engine::soft`.
- `Model`, `Effect`, `Draw`: synthetic turns.
- Simulations:
  - `window` and `chronic`: the chronic tests;
  - `burst_window`: H5a;
  - `event_window_ppm`: the acute count rules;
  - `quarantine`: §6.3;
  - `decay_cap` and `decay_table`: §6.2;
  - `run_agent`: the sentinel's group logic with LIFO rollback, S1 and a clean-only ring;
  - `twin` and `twin_test`;
  - `levels`: §7.2.
- `salami_cell`, `twin_null`, `acceptance`, `report`, `judge_vectors`.

**The clustering model** (for X3's reproduction):
- Turns come in sessions of geometric length, with mean L = 4 (or 8): P(len = l) = (1 − p)^(l−1)·p, p = 1/L.
- Each session draws a shared log offset S ~ N(0, σ_s²), with σ_s = 0.6 (or 0.3).
- Per turn:
  - cost = round(3000·e^(S + σ_t·Z₁)) µUSD;
  - wall = round(40000·e^(S + σ_t·Z₂)) ms;
  - bytes = round(60000·e^(S + σ_t·Z₃));
  - σ_t = √(1 − σ_s²), with Z₁, Z₂, Z₃ independent;
  - requests = max(1, round((1 + U{0..7})·e^S)).
- Errors are Bernoulli(0.02) per request. Corrections are Bernoulli(0.10), independent of S.
- Each window starts a fresh stream with a new session. Its ring is the 40 turns immediately before it. The request
  ring is 400 independent Bernoulli(0.02) draws.
- Code: `Model::sessions(L, σ_s)` and `Draw::turn`.

## Deviations
- "Each group −2% on cost" is read as cost × 1.02 per group. "Within 6 weeks" is reported under both readings (any
  weekly report, and the week-6 report); the verdict uses the strict one because the literal one has a 26% null rate.
- The twin is simulated at the pair level. Each pair is drift + σ_w·(Z₁ − Z₂): the task effect cancels. `ended`,
  McNemar and the budget are not simulated; the budget enters as `k_night`.
- `run_agent` counts owner turns only. Windows close at 20 owner turns, with no clock; background turns are left out
  of H9's request counts.
- Quarantine: a quarantined cap is replaced at once by a fresh cap of the same use rate. This slightly over-counts test
  opportunities, which is conservative.
- Levels: the ring counts as full after 40 owner turns. There are no demotions or quarantines, and the twin's "no
  regression" is a given function.
