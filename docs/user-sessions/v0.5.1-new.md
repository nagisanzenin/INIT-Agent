## User session — v0.5.1 — NEW (a developer who has never used INIT)
PROVENANCE: An AI persona agent (Claude Opus 5.5) played the user. It drove tmux with send-keys and capture-pane only, against the scripted mock model on the test Mac. This can show what a first-time user sees on screen in this flow. It cannot speak for real-model behaviour, real provider billing, the get.sh download (the harness had already installed INIT) or a normal LaunchAgent install.
target: macOS arm64   model: mock (scripted)   real minutes: 44 (17:37 to 18:21 by `date`)

WHAT WORKED
- First-run setup was smooth. I picked the provider, then pasted the key, which showed as dots and came back as "[root] key stored for deepseek (mode 0600; never shown)". Then I picked the model and got "Setup done."
- hello.py: the agent asked "local time or in UTC?" in a picker, and I answered it. Checked from the shell: the file exists and `python3 hello.py` printed "hello" and "today is Wednesday 28 October 2026, 18:18 +07".
- The self-change was kept: "✓ kept 9aa7d39624ce · 4/4 gates · G4 not scored · 32 min 56 s · no restart". The lineage shows "gate accept g0_g3" and "promotion kept (LKG)", with "verify ✓ … every signature". Nothing new appeared in my project folder.
- /undo and /panic both make you type a confirm word first, and the text explaining each one is clear. Panic did what it said: "killed 2 processes in 2 instances; egress closed; level 0→0", and /resume brought it back.
- Ctrl-D quits and prints the total so far ("Metered so far: $0.049 in all").

WHAT CONFUSED ME
- "the output is above" for hello.py, but no output was shown anywhere. Ctrl-O and /details only show "call: set -eu…" and "exit 0, 3 lines". I could not see the command it ran or what it printed.
- The installer contradicts itself. Early on it says "no Rust (--no-rust): INIT will rewrite only its skills and prompts (T0)". Later it says "self-evolution: T0+T1+T2". Which is it?
- /root ledger is not about money. Before and after the kept change it said "last change none · next none"; only "record seq 9" became "seq 15".
- The /undo times are in UTC ("11:19:10Z") while my clock says 18:19.
- I could not find a way to /undo to the first snapshot. "/undo again goes back" just swaps between the last two states.
- During the panic, /status said "the system is restarting; your message is queued (1)". It was not restarting; it was stopped.
- /resume printed "✕ [root] resumed after panic…". The ? help says ✕ means failed, so did the resume fail or not?
- "(held-only build: a process that left its group is reported, not killed)" and "via owner_socket" mean nothing to me.

WHAT ANNOYED ME, and what I would have quit over
- I would have quit about 10–12 minutes into the gate. The panel showed "G0  build + tests □ 12 min 3 s" climbing with no output and no ETA. Afterwards it said "G0 ■ pass · 2 min 32 s · waited 17 min 46 s". So for about 18 minutes the screen showed a running build while it was actually waiting, and it never told me why.
- 33 minutes for a line-count tool. G3 alone took about 12 minutes.
- Panic quietly threw away my new tool. The lineage shows "rollback → tb75a7fb288b4 panic", and afterwards /status says "sha 4c332dd485ce" (the old body). The /panic warning only said it restarts "on its last audited-good version". Nothing told me the tool I had just kept would be dropped.
- /undo did nothing to my folder. When I reopened init-agent, it took a new snapshot ("snapshot 20261028T111910.728Z-project taken (/undo restores it)"), and that snapshot already contained hello.py. After confirming, hello.py was still there. The README says /undo restores the folder; in practice it only goes back to when you last opened init-agent.
- "/undo list" was not treated as a command. It went to the model and was billed: the turn cost $0.001 and the total went from $0.047 to $0.049.
- Ctrl-Z did nothing, and there is no shell escape, so I had to quit INIT to look at my own folder.

WHAT IT TOLD ME vs WHAT WAS TRUE
- **Tokens and requests agree everywhere:**
  - The turns add up (12.0k + 25.2k + 85.2k = 122.4k; 9 requests) and the cache hit checks out (94208/122400, shown as 76.9%).
  - /usage showed "live tokens: input 256289, output 1091" and "$0.047".
  - `init-root egress stats` agreed exactly: "day_spent_musd":47048, "total_input_tokens":256289, "total_output_tokens":1091, "requests_today":25. The TUI counted only 10 requests itself, so 15 came from the gates.
- **Split of the cost:** "self-changes $0.037" against about $0.010 for my chat turns, so the gates cost almost four times my work.
- **I could not check the dollar figure.** The picker shows "$0.30 in · $1.20 out per 1M". At that price, 256289 input tokens alone would be about $0.077, against $0.047 shown. A cache discount must explain the gap, but no cache price is shown anywhere.
- **The cost line jumped ahead of the work:**
  - Before the gates ran, the footer said "$0.005 incl. checks", though the checks later cost about $0.036.
  - After I reopened init-agent, the same turn showed "$0.000 incl. checks" (it had been $0.001).
- **Replayed history is wrong:** after reopening, "ran 5 tools in 0.0 s" (originally "3 min 46 s"), and the transcript says "started 11:19:10Z · 0.0 s" for calls that ran at 10:39.
- **Gate times:** G0 2 min 32 s (plus a 17 min 46 s wait), G1 2.6 s, G2 24 s, G3 last seen at 11 min 57 s. That adds up to the stated 32 min 56 s.
- **After panic, the body disagrees across screens:** the footer says "body 9aa7d39624ce", /status says "sha 4c332dd485ce · lkg 9aa7d39624ce", and the lineage says it rolled back to tb75a7fb288b4.
- **Signing key:** the installer printed "lineage key fingerprint 73b8d88290afeeb79d51ff6d171e678e"; the TUI lineage says "key 4b621f2e748187ed". I cannot tell whether these are the same key.
- **Levels:** "L0 · cap L0 · max L4 · supported L0" was shown and never explained.
- **Paths:** the TUI says the Root files are at "~/.init-root", but this install's Root is /private/tmp/i5-us51-a/r.
- **Small things:** hello.py was created with mode -rw-------. "thought for 0.0 s" and "Mock reasoning." are mock artefacts.

EVERY COMMAND I WAS TOLD TO TYPE
- README `curl … get.sh | sh`: not run by me; the harness had already installed INIT.
- README `init-agent`: failed with "zsh: command not found: init-agent".
- Installer `export PATH="/private/tmp/i5-us51-a/b:$PATH"`: worked, and then `init-agent` opened.
- Installer `echo 'export PATH=…' >> ~/.zshrc`: not run, since it writes outside ~/init-us51-a.
- Installer `/private/tmp/i5-us51-a/b/init-agent`: not run; the PATH route worked.
- Installer `INIT_ROOT_HOME=… init-root start --foreground`: already running in a:root; not touched.
- Installer `INIT_ROOT_HOME=… init-root egress stats`: worked. It prints raw JSON, and the numbers match /usage.
- Installer `init-root keys set deepseek`, `charter check --apply`, `toolchain --set`, `init-agent upgrade`, `uninstall.sh`: not run.
- TUI `/usage`: worked. `/details` and Ctrl-O: worked, but don't show the command or its output. `?`: worked.
- `/status`, `/root help`, `/root ledger`: worked. /root help is a bare list with no explanations.
- `/undo` then `confirm`: worked mechanically, but restored only the snapshot taken when I reopened init-agent.
- `/panic` then `panic`: worked.
- `/resume` (told to me by the panic message): worked, but printed a ✕ and dropped the kept change.
- Ctrl-D: worked, twice.

WOULD A STRANGER GET THROUGH THIS?   no
Setup and the small task are fine. The headline feature, a self-change, sat for about 20 minutes on a screen that said "build + tests" while it was actually waiting, and most people would give up there. Then /panic silently undid the kept change while the footer still showed the new body. /undo also could not remove the file INIT made once I had reopened it. Each of these would cost a stranger's trust in the exact thing INIT claims to do well: check each change and undo it.

VERDICT: do not ship. The fixes:
1. Label the G0 wait as a wait and say why.
2. Make the footer match /status after a rollback.
3. Say before panicking which kept changes will be lost.
4. Make /undo reach the snapshot from before INIT's own edits, or say clearly that it can't.

**Harness artifacts, and what each changed:**
1. **The install was the harness's.**
   - The installer's "no Rust (--no-rust): … (T0)" followed by "self-evolution: T0+T1+T2" comes from `--no-rust` with rustup on PATH: install.sh records the toolchain it finds.
   - A real get.sh run installs Rust and prints the Rust line instead.
   - get.sh's `--no-rust` line is still wrong when rustup is on PATH. Low.
2. **No LaunchAgent: the Root ran in `a:root`.** Hence "the system is restarting; your message is queued" was not checked against the service path.
3. **A test ROOT_HOME.** It put `INIT_ROOT_HOME=…` and test paths into every printed command. "~/.init-root" in the workspace line is wrong only for a non-default ROOT_HOME. Low.
4. **hello.py mode 0600** came from the harness tmux server's umask 077, not INIT's choice.
5. **The scripted model.**
   - "the output is above" is the script's text.
   - "use your new tool…" got a filler reply, so whether the T1 tool works was not tested.
   - The persona's choice ("Local time") matched the script.
6. **Load.** Another release job ran the whole time: bundle-e2e `--t2`, install-matrix and `cargo test`, at a load of about 10 on 8 cores, with 21–27 GB free.
   - G0's cold dependency build (17 min 16 s) and G3 (12 min) are inflated by it. v0.5.0's cold T1 took 17 min 47 s end to end.
   - The durations are an artifact. The labels are not.
7. **A fresh-Mac PATH.** "zsh: command not found: init-agent" is the real state of a default Mac, not an artifact. get.sh's last line ("for this terminal: export PATH=…") and install.sh's `echo … >> ~/.zshrc` line both give the fix, and the persona recovered from the screen in one step.

**Product findings** (CONFIRMED = on the Root's records or in code; PLAUSIBLE = from the screen only), by severity:
